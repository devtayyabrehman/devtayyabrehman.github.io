Overview

A new DTC skincare brand had a great product but no way to scale beyond word of mouth. They had tried Meta ads previously with poor results.

Key aspects

01 / The Challenge

What stood in the way

Growth Stagnation & Scaling Friction

Their previous ad attempts were disjointed. They were running single-image ads to cold traffic with no follow-up funnel, resulting in high CPMs and low lifetime value.

02 / The Approach

What changed

We built a full-funnel Meta Ads machine. Using a 'Broad Targeting' approach at the top to let the algorithm find the audience, and sophisticated dynamic remarketing at the bottom. We also implemented a weekly creative testing cycle to combat ad fatigue.
